# Refine reference — the checkpoint and recommendation

Follow this when the product owner asks "where are we", and at the end of an epic. One shape, every time, so the
product owner always knows what is done, what is next and what you recommend.

## The shape

1. **Where we are.** One line that answers the question. Lead with it.
2. **The plan, as a table.** Columns: `#`, work, state. States: ✅ shipped (PR) · ⬜ not started · in flight.
   Build it from the build order and the epic READMEs, never from memory.
3. **What stands between us and the goal.** Plainly: what is left, what is blocked, and on which step.
4. **Seeds planted along the way that need folding in.** Each with a one-line recommendation: fold in · after
   launch · drop.
5. **One recommendation, ordered, and ONE question.** The recommendation is the next steps in order. The question
   is the single thing you need from the product owner to proceed.

## Rules

- **Lead with the answer.** No preamble, no history first.
- **Plain words.** No internal file paths unless the product owner asks for them.
- **Say what is owed to the product owner by the name of the step**, not by blame ("the sign-in walkthrough is
  owed", not "you haven't done it").
- **Never invent a state.** Read it: the build order, the epic READMEs, the PRs. If you cannot read it, say so.
