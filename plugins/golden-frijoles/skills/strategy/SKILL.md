One skill, four chapters. Each chapter is a full coach in `references/`; read the one the person asked for and follow
it exactly, from its first line. Paths in a chapter (`templates/…`, `references/…`) are relative to this skill's base
directory. The chapters hand off to each other in this order, and any of them can be run on its own.

| # | Chapter | Read | Leaves | Pick it when the person asks for… |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Cold read** (optional, first) | `references/cold-read.md` | `Roadmap/00-strategy/cold-read/` (sealed) | a cold read, a blind run, an independent read, a second opinion before coaching |
| 1 | **PMF narrative** | `references/pmf-narrative.md` | `Roadmap/00-strategy/pmf-narrative.md` | a product strategy, a PMF narrative, why the product should exist |
| 2 | **North Star** | `references/north-star.md` | `Roadmap/00-strategy/north-star.md` | a North Star, input metrics, leading indicators, the one metric to grow |
| 3 | **Risk validation** | `references/risk-validation.md` | `Roadmap/00-strategy/risk-validation.md` | what to validate first, the riskiest assumption, how to de-risk before building |

**Which chapter, when nothing was named:** look in `Roadmap/00-strategy/`. No `pmf-narrative.md` → chapter 1 (offer chapter 0 first, once). A narrative but no
`north-star.md` → chapter 2. Both but no `risk-validation.md` → chapter 3. All three → ask which one to revisit.

**One chapter at a time:** finish the chapter you started (its last step offers the next one) rather
than mixing two. The coaching rules every chapter follows are in refine's `references/coaching.md`; each chapter says
so in its first lines.

This skill was four skills before 1.0 (`cold-read`, `pmf-narrative`, `north-star`, `risk-validation`). A person who
asks for one of those by name means the chapter of the same name.
